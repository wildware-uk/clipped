# NNNN. Short title stating the decision

- Status: `Proposed` | `Accepted` | `Superseded by NNNN`, linked to the record
  that replaces it
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Issue: the issue this decision was made under

## Context

The forces in play, not the answer. What problem is being solved, what
constraints apply, what has to remain true afterwards. Cite the requirement
where one exists — SPEC.md section N, AGENTS.md section N, an issue — so a
reader can check whether the constraint still holds.

State what is *not* in scope if that is what keeps the decision small.

## Decision

What was chosen, in a sentence or two of plain language. Then the detail a
contributor needs in order to apply it: what it means in practice, and where the
boundary of the decision lies.

## Alternatives

Each alternative that was seriously considered, with its real case made before
it is rejected.

### Alternative name

What it is, what it would have given us, and the specific reason it was not
chosen. "It is worse" is not a reason; "it loses the whole recording on an
abrupt termination" is.

If an alternative is close, say so, and say what would make it win later. That
is the sentence a future contributor needs when circumstances change.

## Consequences

What follows from this, good and bad. Include:

- what becomes easy;
- what becomes hard or impossible;
- what work this creates, with an issue or milestone where one exists;
- what has to be watched or measured to know whether the decision still holds.

An entry with only benefits means the trade-off has not been found yet.
